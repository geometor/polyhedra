Exact Algebraic Coordinates & Geometric Proof¶

The Seked 14:11 Pyramid embodies the historical architectural profile of the Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu), recorded in the Rhind Mathematical Papyrus (Problem 56). A seked of \(5 \frac{1}{2}\) palms gives a rise-to-run ratio of \(14 : 11\) (semi-base \(b = 1\), height \(h = 14/11\)).

Coordinates in origin-centered space with base side 2:

  • Base Vertices (4): \((\pm 1, \pm 1, -\frac{7}{22})\) with quadrance \(Q_v = \frac{1017}{484}\).

  • Apex (1): \((0, 0, \frac{21}{22})\) with quadrance \(Q_v = \frac{441}{484}\).

Circle Quadrature Harmony: The perimeter of the square base is \(4 \times 2 = 8\). Under the canonical Egyptian approximation \(\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}\):

\[2 \pi h = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{14}{11} = 8 = \text{Perimeter}_{\text{base}}\]

The base edge quadrance is \(Q_b = 4\), the hip edge quadrance is \(Q_e = \frac{438}{121}\), and the dihedral spread is the strictly rational fraction \(S = \frac{85848}{100489}\).
