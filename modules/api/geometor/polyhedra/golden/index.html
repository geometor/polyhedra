geometor.polyhedra.golden¶

Exact symbolic golden proportions and Keplerian compounds in polyhedra.

Explores canonical golden ratio occurrences across regular and semi-regular polyhedra: mutually orthogonal golden rectangles in the icosahedron, golden edge division of the octahedron, the compound of five inscribed cubes in the dodecahedron, Kepler’s rhombic triacontahedron, and planar cross sections (George Odom’s theorem, icosahedron principal meridian, and regular pentagon/pentagram).

geometor.polyhedra.golden.build_icosahedron_golden_rectangles() → GoldenRectanglesResult¶

Extracts and certifies the three mutually orthogonal golden rectangles in the icosahedron.

Euclid Elements XIII.16 shows that the twelve vertices of a regular icosahedron form the vertices of three congruent golden rectangles whose planes are pairwise perpendicular and meet at the center of the circumscribing sphere.

Returns:

GoldenRectanglesResult certifying rectangle dimensions, orthogonality, and edges.

geometor.polyhedra.golden.build_octahedron_golden_icosahedron() → OctahedronGoldenIcosahedronResult¶

Divides the twelve edges of a regular octahedron in the golden ratio.

Starts with a regular octahedron of scale phi^2 = phi + 1 whose vertices lie along the coordinate axes: (±phi^2, 0, 0), (0, ±phi^2, 0), (0, 0, ±phi^2). Dividing each directed edge in the golden ratio AG : GB = 1 : phi yields the exact twelve vertices of the canonical regular icosahedron.

Returns:

OctahedronGoldenIcosahedronResult containing octahedron geometry and inscribed icosahedron.

geometor.polyhedra.golden.build_dodecahedron_five_cubes() → DodecahedronFiveCubesResult¶

Finds and certifies all five regular cubes inscribed in the dodecahedron.

Euclid Elements XIII.17 constructs the regular dodecahedron upon an inscribed cube by adding roof-like wedges above each square face. There are five distinct inscribed regular cubes whose 60 edges partition all 60 diagonals of the twelve pentagonal faces.

Returns:

DodecahedronFiveCubesResult containing dodecahedron geometry and the five cubes.

geometor.polyhedra.golden.build_rhombic_triacontahedron_golden() → RhombicTriacontahedronResult¶

Constructs the exact 32-vertex Rhombic Triacontahedron over Q(phi).

Unites the twenty vertices of the canonical regular dodecahedron with the twelve vertices of the dual-oriented regular icosahedron. Each of the thirty faces is a planar golden rhombus whose long diagonal joins two acute degree-5 icosahedral vertices and whose short diagonal joins two obtuse degree-3 dodecahedral vertices, with diagonal quadrance ratio equal to phi^2.

Returns:

RhombicTriacontahedronResult specifying vertices, rhombic faces, and metric ratios.

geometor.polyhedra.golden.build_odom_tetrahedron_section_model() → tuple[geometor.model.Model, dict[str, sympy.Expr]]¶

Constructs George Odom’s golden section on the regular tetrahedron face circumcircle.

In 1983, George Odom discovered that connecting the midpoints of two sides of an equilateral triangle inscribed in a circle and extending the line to meet the circle divides the chord in the exact golden ratio.

Returns:

Tuple of (Model, metrics_dict) with certified exact golden proportions.

geometor.polyhedra.golden.build_icosahedron_meridian_model() → tuple[geometor.model.Model, dict[str, sympy.Expr]]¶

Constructs the exact 2D principal meridian section of the icosahedron.

In the principal plane X = 0, the canonical regular icosahedron has four vertices forming a golden rectangle (±1, ±phi) and two equatorial crossings at (±phi, 0) in (Y, Z) coordinates.

Returns:

Tuple of (Model, metrics_dict) certifying golden proportions in the meridian slice.

geometor.polyhedra.golden.build_pentagon_cross_section_model() → tuple[geometor.model.Model, dict[str, sympy.Expr]]¶

Constructs the regular pentagon and pentagram section over Q(phi, sqrt(5 - sqrt(5))).

Euclid Elements XIII.8 demonstrates that the diagonals of a regular pentagon cut one another in extreme and mean ratio (the golden ratio). This configuration represents the vertex figure of the icosahedron and the face of the dodecahedron.

Returns:

Tuple of (Model, metrics_dict) certifying pentagon-pentagram golden proportions.
