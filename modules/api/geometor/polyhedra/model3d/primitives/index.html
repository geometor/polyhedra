geometor.polyhedra.model3d.primitives¶

Exact symbolic 3D geometric primitives and polyhedral models.

Provides 100% exact algebraic representations of vectors, points, lines, planes, spheres, and polyhedral solids over Q, Q(phi), and radical extensions, with zero float or sp.Float coordinates.
