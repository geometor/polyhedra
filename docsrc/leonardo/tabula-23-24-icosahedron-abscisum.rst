Tabulae XXIII–XXIIII: Icosahedron Abscisum — The Fullerene (1509)
======================================================================

*The Truncated Icosahedron in Solid and Hollow Perspective by Leonardo da Vinci*

.. epigraph::

   *Icosahedron abscisum solidum fit quando a singulis angulis icosahedri abscinditur portio unius tertiae partis laterum confluentium...*

   *The solid truncated icosahedron is made when from each angle of the icosahedron a portion of one-third part of the converging edges is cut away...*

   — **Luca Pacioli**, *De Divina Proportione*, Cap. XXXVII (Venice, 1509)

.. enunciation::

   When the 12 vertices of a regular icosahedron are truncated at rational parameter :math:`t = 1/3`, the 12 pentagonal corners become 12 regular pentagons, and the 20 triangular faces become 20 regular hexagons. Leonardo da Vinci illustrates this Archimedean solid in **Tabula XXIII (Folio 0235)** as *Icosahedron Abscisum Solidum* and in **Tabula XXIIII (Folio 0237)** as *Icosahedron Abscisum Vacuum* (genus :math:`g = 31`). This pair represents the **first published illustration in world history** of the 32-face geometry later recognized as the soccer ball and the carbon allotrope Buckminsterfullerene (:math:`\text{C}_{60}`).

Leonardo as the First Illustrator of the Buckyball
--------------------------------------------------

The truncated icosahedron possesses:

- **Vertices**: :math:`V = 2E_{\text{ico}} = 2(30) = 60`
- **Edges**: :math:`E = 3(30) = 90`
- **Faces**: :math:`F = 12 \text{ pentagons} + 20 \text{ hexagons} = 32`

This is the exact geometric architecture of the classical association football (soccer ball) and the Nobel Prize-winning molecule **Buckminsterfullerene** (:math:`\text{C}_{60}`).

**Leonardo da Vinci was the very first person in history to draw and publish this 32-face cage**—four hundred and seventy-six years before Kroto, Smalley, and Curl synthesized :math:`\text{C}_{60}` in 1985.

In Tabula XXIII, Leonardo renders the alternating rhythm of pentagons and hexagons with spherical volume.

In Tabula XXIIII, the hollow carpentry lattice creates a through-hole at every face (12 pentagonal windows and 20 hexagonal windows), yielding a manifold of genus :math:`g = 31`.

Topological Invariants of the Hollow Framework
----------------------------------------------

By the Vacuate Manifold Theorem, the hollow truncated icosahedron satisfies:

- **Base Solid Invariants**: :math:`V = 60`, :math:`E = 90`, :math:`F = 32` (12 pentagons, 20 hexagons)
- **Hollow Frame Vertices**: :math:`V_{\text{vac}} = 2V + 4E = 2(60) + 4(90) = 480`
- **Hollow Frame Edges**: :math:`E_{\text{vac}} = 12E = 12(90) = 1080`
- **Hollow Frame Faces**: :math:`F_{\text{vac}} = 6E = 6(90) = 540` planar trapezoids
- **Euler Characteristic**: :math:`\chi_{\text{vac}} = 480 - 1080 + 540 = -60`
- **Topological Genus**: :math:`g = F - 1 = 32 - 1 = 31`
- **Coordinate Field**: Quadratic extension field :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)`
