Exact Algebraic Coordinates & Geometric Proof¶

The Cuboctahedron Elevatum (Exahedron Abscisum Elevatum) is Pacioli’s 14-base stellation formed by elevating the 8 triangular and 6 square faces of the Archimedean cuboctahedron. Starting from the cuboctahedron with 12 vertices at permutations of \((\pm 1, \pm 1, 0)\) (edge length \(s = \sqrt{2}\)):

  • Triangular Apices (8): \(h_3 = \sqrt{2 - \frac{2}{3}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\). Normal \((1, 1, 1)\) yields apex \((2/3, 2/3, 2/3) + 2/3(1, 1, 1) = (4/3, 4/3, 4/3)\).

  • Square Apices (6): \(h_4 = \sqrt{2 - 1} = 1\). Normal \((1, 0, 0)\) yields apex \((1, 0, 0) + 1 \cdot (1, 0, 0) = (2, 0, 0)\).

Both sets of apices have strictly rational coordinates! Thus all 26 vertices lie entirely in \(\mathbb{Q}\). The manifold contains 48 equilateral triangular faces with genus \(g = 47\).
