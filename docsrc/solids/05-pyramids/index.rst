Architectural Profiles and Rational Trigonometry
------------------------------------------------

Unlike general polyhedra, pyramids are oriented manifolds defined by a polygonal base in a reference plane and an apex point at perpendicular altitude :math:`h`. The GEOMETOR architecture formulates their geometry through exact Rational Trigonometry:

- **Base Edge Quadrance (:math:`Q_b`)**: Squared base side length :math:`s^2`.
- **Slant Edge Quadrance (:math:`Q_e`)**: Squared distance from apex to base vertex :math:`h^2 + r_{\text{base}}^2`.
- **Slope Spread (:math:`S_{\text{slope}}`)**: The squared sine of the face inclination:

  .. math::

     S_{\text{slope}} = \frac{h^2}{h^2 + b^2}

- **Primary Dihedral Spread (:math:`S_{\text{dihedral}}`)**: The exact spread between adjacent triangular faces meeting at a lateral hip edge.

The Four Canonical Pyramids
---------------------------

1. **The Kepler Golden Pyramid (P-05.01)**:
   Conceived by **Johannes Kepler** in 1597 and historically identified with **Herodotus**'s account of the Great Pyramid (*Histories* II.124). Its cross-section is the Kepler right triangle with side ratio :math:`1 : \sqrt{\phi} : \phi`. The altitude squared equals the area of each lateral triangular face: :math:`h^2 = (\sqrt{\phi})^2 = \phi = \text{Area}(\triangle)`. Its number field is the radical tower :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\phi})`.

2. **The Egyptian Seked 14:11 Pyramid (P-05.02)**:
   The empirical architectural profile of the Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu), documented in the Rhind Mathematical Papyrus (Problem 56) with a seked of :math:`5 \frac{1}{2}` palms (rise 14, run 11). This strictly rational profile (:math:`\mathbb{Q}`) achieves the classical quadrature of the circle: the perimeter of the base :math:`4 \times 2 = 8` equals the circumference of a circle of radius :math:`h = \frac{14}{11}` under the Archimedean approximation :math:`\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}`:

   .. math::

      2 \pi h = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{14}{11} = 8 = \text{Perimeter}_{\text{base}}

3. **The Golden Square Bipyramid (P-05.03)**:
   Formed by reflecting the Kepler Golden Pyramid across its square base, creating an 8-face octahedral double pyramid with :math:`D_{4h}` symmetry. It preserves Kepler's primary dihedral spread :math:`3\phi - 4` across all 12 edges.

4. **The Regular Pentagonal Pyramid (Johnson J2, P-05.04)**:
   The equilateral pentagonal pyramid comprising 1 regular pentagon and 5 equilateral triangles. Cataloged by **Norman Johnson** (1966) as solid :math:`J_2`, it is the 6-vertex cap severed from the regular icosahedron in Euclid *Elements* XIII.16. Its 6 vertices embed directly in :math:`\mathbb{Z}[\phi]` on the cutting plane :math:`y + \phi z = \phi`.
