Polyhedra¶

Quinque corpora regularia sunt: tetraedron, exaedron sive cubus, octaedron, icosaedron, et dodecaedron. Quorum quodlibet intra sphaeram comprehendi potest.

There are five regular solids: the tetrahedron, hexahedron or cube, octahedron, icosahedron, and dodecahedron. Every one of them can be comprehended within a sphere.

—Luca Pacioli, De Divina Proportione (Venice, 1509)

Overview¶

The mathematical catalog models three-dimensional constructive geometry, regular and semi-regular polyhedra, and spatial proportion in 100% exact symbolic algebra using SymPy, Rational Trigonometry dihedral spreads, and exact number fields. Within the interconnected GEOMETOR semantic digital archive, classical treatises—from Euclid’s Elements Book XIII and Plato’s Timaeus to Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione and Johannes Kepler’s Harmonices Mundi—are liberated into 26 exact symbolic solids, interactive 3D HTML5 viewports, theme-adaptive vector diagrams, and Leonardo da Vinci woodcut folios speaking one shared geometric language.

  • Catalog of Polyhedra — Master Polyhedra Catalog (26 Solids, One Polyhedron Per Page) organized into 5 collection families with inline SVG cards, interactive 3D Solidum / Vacuum studios, unfolded 2D nets, and Master Comparative Ledger.

  • Golden Proportions in Polyhedra — Collection of certified Golden Ratio (\(\phi\)) theorems across 3D polyhedra and 2D cross-sections: Golden Rectangles of the Icosahedron, Golden Division of the Octahedron, Euclid XIII.17 & the Five Cubes in the Dodecahedron, Kepler’s Rhombic Triacontahedron, and Odom’s Tetrahedron Section.

  • Leonardo da Vinci’s Polyhedral Plates (1509) — Visual museum collection of 20 paired 1509 woodcut folio studies by Leonardo da Vinci from Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione, paired with exact genus \(g = F - 1\) Corpora Vacua proofs.

  • Kepler, Squaring the Circle & the Great Pyramid — Collection of treatises on Kepler, Squaring the Circle & the Great Pyramid: The 1597 Kepler Right Triangle (\(1, \sqrt{\phi}, \phi\); \(A_{\text{face}} = h^2 = \phi\)), Squaring the Circle & the Earth–Moon Cosmogram (\(8 = 2\pi h\), \(\pi_{\phi} = 4/\sqrt{\phi}\), \(r_M/r_E = 3/11\)), and Egyptian 14:11 Seked Mensuration & Polyhedral Spreads.

  • 3D Symbolic Infrastructure & Multi-Engine Pipeline — Architecture of geometor.polyhedra.model3d, Conway/Pacioli operators (vacuate, rectify, truncate, elevate, polar_dual), and multi-engine exports to 2D SVG, HTML5 3D Canvas, IngeTrazo 0.6.0, and Blender 5.x.
