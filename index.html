polyhedra¶

Exact Symbolic Platonic Solids, Golden Proportions, 3D Rational Trigonometry, and Leonardo da Vinci’s Polyhedral Woodcuts

Quinque corpora regularia sunt: tetraedron, exaedron sive cubus, octaedron, icosaedron, et dodecaedron. Quorum quodlibet intra sphaeram comprehendi potest.

There are five regular solids: the tetrahedron, hexahedron or cube, octahedron, icosahedron, and dodecahedron. Every one of them can be comprehended within a sphere.

—Luca Pacioli, De Divina Proportione (Venice, 1509)

Overview¶

GEOMETOR Polyhedra (geometor-polyhedra) models three-dimensional constructive geometry and regular solids in 100% exact symbolic algebra (zero floating-point approximations) using SymPy and geometor.model.

Grounding its algebraic foundations in Euclid’s Elements Book XIII, Plato’s Timaeus, Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione (1509), Johannes Kepler’s Strena (1611), and Norman Wildberger’s Rational Trigonometry, this edition unites classical geometric scholarship with symbolic computation over \(\mathbb{Z}\) and \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) (\(\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}\)).

Book Codices & Architecture¶

  • The Five Platonic Solids — Dedicated monographs for the Tetrahedron, Hexahedron, Octahedron, Icosahedron, and Dodecahedron, with Euclid Book XIII constructions, pure rational dihedral spreads, interactive 3D solids, and non-overlapping 2D unfolded nets.

  • Golden Proportions in Polyhedra — Six certified Golden Ratio ($\phi$) theorems across 3D polyhedra and 2D cross-sections: Golden Rectangles of the Icosahedron, Golden Division of the Octahedron, Euclid XIII.17 & the Five Cubes in the Dodecahedron, and Kepler’s Rhombic Triacontahedron & Odom’s Tetrahedron Section.

  • Leonardo da Vinci’s Polyhedral Plates (1509) — Side-by-side Digital Scriptorium study of 38 original 1509 woodcut plates by Leonardo da Vinci (Tabulae I–XX and Tabulae XXI–XXXVI) paired with exact genus $g = F - 1$ Corpora Vacua reconstructions.

  • 3D Symbolic Infrastructure & Multi-Engine Pipeline — Architecture of geometor.polyhedra.model3d, Conway/Pacioli operators (vacuate, rectify, truncate, elevate, polar_dual), and multi-engine exports to 2D SVG, HTML5 3D Canvas, IngeTrazo 0.6.0, and Blender 5.x.

  • Modules · Log · Changelog — Symbolic Python API reference, research log, and release history.
