===============================================================================
Tabulae XXXIII–XXXIIII: Dodecahedron Abscisum Elevatum Solidum et Vacuum (1509)
===============================================================================

*The Compound Elevated Icosidodecahedron in Solid and Hollow Perspective by Leonardo da Vinci*

.. epigraph::

   *Dodecahedron abscisum elevatum solidum est corpus compositum super triginta duabus basibus abscisi dodecahedri habens totidem pyramides erectas...*

   *The solid elevated truncated dodecahedron is a composite body upon the thirty-two bases of the truncated dodecahedron having as many pyramids erected...*

   — **Luca Pacioli**, *De Divina Proportione*, Cap. XLII (Venice, 1509)

.. enunciation::

   By erecting regular pyramids over all 32 facets of the icosidodecahedron (12 pentagonal pyramids and 20 triangular pyramids), Leonardo da Vinci constructs the magnificent compound solid **Dodecahedron Abscisum Elevatum** (*Icosidodecahedron Elevatum*), consisting of :math:`12 \times 5 + 20 \times 3 = 120` triangular facets, 180 edges, and 62 vertices. In Luca Pacioli's *De Divina Proportione* (1509), this masterpiece is illustrated in **Tabula XXXIII (Folio 0255)** as *Dodecahedron Abscisum Elevatum Solidum* and in **Tabula XXXIIII (Folio 0257)** as *Dodecahedron Abscisum Elevatum Vacuum* (genus :math:`g = 119`).

Monumental Stellation & The Climax of Regular Forms
---------------------------------------------------

The icosidodecahedron elevatum represents the most geometrically intricate star polyhedron illustrated in *De Divina Proportione*.

In Tabula XXXIII, Leonardo balances the shading across 120 triangular facets, maintaining clear volumetric coherence across thirty-two separate pyramidal clusters.

In Tabula XXXIIII, the vacuate model reaches the extreme limit of woodcut perspective illustration: an open carpentry network with 120 triangular window apertures and 180 mitered wooden rails, producing a closed 2-manifold surface of topological genus :math:`g = 119`.

Topological Invariants of the Hollow Framework
----------------------------------------------

By the Vacuate Manifold Theorem, the hollow compound elevated icosidodecahedron satisfies:

- **Base Solid Invariants**: :math:`V = 30 + 12 + 20 = 62`, :math:`E = 60 + 60 + 60 = 180`, :math:`F = 60 + 60 = 120` triangular facets
- **Hollow Frame Vertices**: :math:`V_{\text{vac}} = 2V + 4E = 2(62) + 4(180) = 844`
- **Hollow Frame Edges**: :math:`E_{\text{vac}} = 12E = 12(180) = 2160`
- **Hollow Frame Faces**: :math:`F_{\text{vac}} = 6E = 6(180) = 1080` planar trapezoids
- **Euler Characteristic**: :math:`\chi_{\text{vac}} = 844 - 2160 + 1080 = -236`
- **Topological Genus**: :math:`g = F - 1 = 120 - 1 = 119`
- **Coordinate Field**: Quadratic extension field :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)`
