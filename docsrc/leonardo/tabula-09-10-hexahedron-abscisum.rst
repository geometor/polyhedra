============================================================
Tabulae VIIII–X: Exahedron Abscisum Solidum et Vacuum (1509)
============================================================

*The Cuboctahedron in Solid and Hollow Perspective by Leonardo da Vinci*

.. epigraph::

   *Exahedron abscisum solidum fit quando a singulis angulis cubi abscinditur portio usque ad medietates laterum confluentium...*

   *The solid truncated hexahedron is made when from each angle of the cube a portion is cut away up to the midpoints of the converging edges...*

   — **Luca Pacioli**, *De Divina Proportione*, Cap. XXX (Venice, 1509)

.. enunciation::

   By truncating the regular hexahedron (cube) at the **exact midpoints of its twelve edges** (Pacioli's edge rectification), the 8 cube corners are replaced by 8 equilateral triangles, while the 6 square faces shrink into 6 rotated square facets. The resulting Archimedean semi-regular solid is the **Cuboctahedron** (:math:`V = 12, E = 24, F = 14`), illustrated by Leonardo da Vinci in **Tabula VIIII (Folio 0203)** as *Exahedron Abscisum Solidum* and in **Tabula X (Folio 0205)** as *Exahedron Abscisum Vacuum* (genus :math:`g = 13`).

Rectification & Vector Equilibrium
----------------------------------

In modern polyhedral geometry and synergetics (Buckminster Fuller), the cuboctahedron is celebrated as the **Vector Equilibrium**: its 12 vertices lie at unit distance from the origin and the distance between adjacent vertices is also unit, balancing radial and circumferential forces.

In 1509, Luca Pacioli defined this solid as *Exahedron Abscisum*, noting that it shares the dual symmetry of both the cube and the octahedron.

In Tabula VIIII, Leonardo captures the alternating sequence of triangles and squares with dramatic cross-hatching, conveying the planar stability of the 14 facets.

In Tabula X, the vacuate carpentry framework features 14 open window reveals (8 triangular apertures and 6 square apertures) framed by 24 mitered wooden rails.

Topological Invariants of the Hollow Framework
----------------------------------------------

By the Vacuate Manifold Theorem, the hollow cuboctahedron framework satisfies:

- **Base Solid Invariants**: :math:`V = 12`, :math:`E = 24`, :math:`F = 14` (8 triangles, 6 squares)
- **Hollow Frame Vertices**: :math:`V_{\text{vac}} = 2V + 4E = 2(12) + 4(24) = 120`
- **Hollow Frame Edges**: :math:`E_{\text{vac}} = 12E = 12(24) = 288`
- **Hollow Frame Faces**: :math:`F_{\text{vac}} = 6E = 6(24) = 144` planar trapezoids
- **Euler Characteristic**: :math:`\chi_{\text{vac}} = 120 - 288 + 144 = -24`
- **Topological Genus**: :math:`g = F - 1 = 14 - 1 = 13`
- **Coordinate Field**: Pure rational field :math:`\mathbb{Q}`
