geometor.polyhedra.platonic
===========================

.. py:module:: geometor.polyhedra.platonic

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Exact symbolic Platonic solids registry and 3D Rational Trigonometry.

   Provides canonical definitions of the five regular polyhedra over Z and Q(phi)
   with exact coordinates, face orientations, edge topologies, and dihedral spreads.

.. py:class:: RegularSolid

   Exact symbolic specification of a regular convex Platonic solid.

   :ivar slug: Short lowercase identifier ('tetrahedron', 'hexahedron', etc.).
   :ivar name: Standard English name ('Tetrahedron', 'Hexahedron', etc.).
   :ivar latin_name: Classical Latin name from Pacioli, Kepler, and Renaissance texts.
   :ivar greek_name: Ancient Greek name with polytonic Greek orthography.
   :ivar element: Classical natural element assigned in Plato's Timaeus.
   :ivar schlafli: Schläfli symbol {p, q} indicating p-gons with q meeting per vertex.
   :ivar dual_slug: Slug of the polar dual regular solid.
   :ivar euclid_prop: Proposition in Euclid's Elements Book XIII constructing the solid.
   :ivar g_index: GEOMETOR G-Index citation in elements canon.
   :ivar leonardo_plates: Mapping of variant keys to (plate_roman, folio_number) pairs.
   :ivar vertices: Tuple of exact 3D vertex coordinates over Z or Q(phi).
   :ivar faces: Tuple of oriented planar faces (vertex index cycles CCW outward).
   :ivar edges: Tuple of undirected edge pairs (i, j) with i < j.
   :ivar dihedral_spread: Exact rational dihedral spread sin^2(theta).
   :ivar q_in_over_q_out: Exact ratio of inquadrance to circumquadrance Q_in / Q_out.
   :ivar q_mid_over_q_out: Exact ratio of midquadrance to circumquadrance Q_mid / Q_out.

.. py:function:: dihedral_spread_3d(n1: tuple[sympy.Expr, sympy.Expr, sympy.Expr], n2: tuple[sympy.Expr, sympy.Expr, sympy.Expr]) -> sympy.Expr

   Computes the exact dihedral spread (sin^2 theta) between two face normals.

   In Rational Trigonometry, the spread between two planes with normal
   vectors n1 and n2 is 1 - (n1 . n2)^2 / (Q(n1) * Q(n2)).

   :param n1: First face normal vector.
   :param n2: Second face normal vector.

   :returns: Exact rational or algebraic dihedral spread in [0, 1].
