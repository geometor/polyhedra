P-04.05 · Dodecahedron Elevatum¶

Dodecahedron Elevatum · Field: :math:`mathbb{Q}(sqrt{5 - sqrt{5}})` · Symmetry: I_h (order 120) · Non-Convex

A stellated polyhedron formed by erecting equilateral pentagonal pyramids on the twelve faces of a dodecahedron.

Exact Algebraic Coordinates & Geometric Proof¶

The Dodecahedron Elevatum (Pentakis Dodecahedron) is formed by erecting equilateral pentagonal pyramids on the 12 pentagonal faces of the regular dodecahedron. For a regular pentagonal face of side \(s = \sqrt{6 - 2\sqrt{5}}\):

  • Pentagon Circumradius Squared: \(r_5^2 = \frac{s^2}{5 - \sqrt{5}}\).

  • Pyramid Altitude: \(h_5 = s \sqrt{\frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{10}}\).

Erecting 12 pentagonal pyramids produces 12 apices, yielding \(V = 20 + 12 = 32\) vertices, \(E = 90\) edges, and \(F = 60\) equilateral triangular faces. The algebraic number field is the nested radical field \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5 - \sqrt{5}})\). Its topological genus is \(g = 60 - 1 = 59\).
