Changelog¶

0.2.0 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Added exact 3D symbolic modeling kernel geometor.polyhedra.model3d (Point3D, Vector3D, Line3D, Plane3D, Sphere3D, Solid3D) plus 2D Dimensional Bridge (slice_solid_to_model() and unfold_net_2d()).

  • Implemented exact Conway/Pacioli/Leonardo polyhedral operators in geometor.polyhedra.operators: vacuate() (genus \(g = F - 1\) hollow wooden manifold), rectify(), truncate(), elevate() (equilateral stellations including Stella Octangula), and polar_dual().

  • Added geometor.polyhedra.golden proving six canonical Golden Proportion theorems across 3D polyhedra and 2D cross-sections (3 orthogonal golden rectangles in the icosahedron, golden division of the 12 octahedron edges, Euclid XIII.17 compound of 5 cubes in the dodecahedron, Kepler’s 30-face rhombic triacontahedron, Odom’s tetrahedron section, meridian hexagon, and equatorial pentagon/pentagram).

  • Built multi-backend visualization & CAD export suite geometor.polyhedra.render (.euclid-graphic 3D solid/vacuum SVGs, 2D unfolded net SVGs, 2D section SVGs, Wavefront .obj/.mtl, IngeTrazo 0.6.0 live builder, and Blender 5.x bpy studio script) plus zero-dependency interactive HTML5 <canvas> 3D Book Codex engine (polyhedra_3d_codex.js).

  • Authored 15 dedicated :layout: book codex pages across docsrc/platonic/, docsrc/golden/, docsrc/leonardo/ (with 38 curated 1509 Leonardo da Vinci woodcut plates), and docsrc/pipeline/.

0.1.0 (2026-11-14)¶

  • Initial release of geometor-polyhedra (v0.1.0) strictly adhering to GEOMETOR exact symbolic standards (zero float / sp.Float).

  • Implemented exact symbolic Platonic solids registry in geometor.polyhedra.platonic (RegularSolid) with all 5 regular solids (Tetrahedron, Hexahedron, Octahedron, Icosahedron, Dodecahedron) over \(\mathbb{Z}\) and \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\).

  • Defined exact 3D Rational Trigonometry vector helpers: quadrance_3d(), cross_3d(), dot_3d(), face_normal_3d(), and dihedral_spread_3d().

  • Verified polar-duality ratios (\(Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = 1/3\) for Cube and Octahedron, \(Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = (4\phi+3)/15 = 1/3 + 2\sqrt{5}/15\) for Dodecahedron and Icosahedron, \(Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = 1/9\) for self-dual Tetrahedron), all rational dihedral spreads (\(8/9, 1, 8/9, 4/5, 4/9\)), Schläfli counts (\(pF = 2E = qV\)), and Euler characteristic \(V - E + F = 2\).

  • Linked all 60 Leonardo da Vinci polyhedral plates from Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione (1509) across primary variants (solidum, vacuum, abscisum, elevatum).

  • Added polyhedra Rich CLI and runnable demonstration in demos/demo_platonic.py.

  • Authored multi-track Book Codex in docsrc/ with Sphinx AutoAPI reference, Leonardo plates guide, and local intersphinx cross-links.
