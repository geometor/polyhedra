============================================================
Tabulae XI–XII: Exahedron Elevatum Solidum et Vacuum (1509)
============================================================

*The Tetrakis Hexahedron in Solid and Hollow Perspective by Leonardo da Vinci*

.. epigraph::

   *Exahedron elevatum solidum est quando super qualibet sex basium cubi erigitur una pyramis quadrata...*

   *The solid elevated hexahedron is when upon each of the six bases of the cube a square pyramid is erected...*

   — **Luca Pacioli**, *De Divina Proportione*, Cap. XXXI (Venice, 1509)

.. enunciation::

   By erecting square pyramids over the 6 square faces of the regular hexahedron (cube), Leonardo da Vinci produces the **Tetrakis Hexahedron** (*Exahedron Elevatum*), consisting of :math:`6 \times 4 = 24` isosceles triangular faces, 36 edges, and :math:`8 + 6 = 14` vertices. In Luca Pacioli's *De Divina Proportione* (1509), this elevated solid is illustrated in **Tabula XI (Folio 0207)** as *Exahedron Elevatum Solidum* and in **Tabula XII (Folio 0209)** as *Exahedron Elevatum Vacuum* (genus :math:`g = 23`).

Pyramidal Stellation & Geometric Form
-------------------------------------

The tetrakis hexahedron represents the stellation of the cube and the dual of the truncated octahedron. In Pacioli's terminology, the solid is formed *cum pyramidibus superpositis* (with superimposed pyramids).

In Tabula XI, Leonardo renders the acute pyramidal peaks protruding in the six cardinal directions, using chiaroscuro to distinguish the illuminated slopes of the top and left pyramids from the cast shadows on the lower right pyramids.

In Tabula XII, the vacuate structure exhibits 24 triangular window apertures bordered by 36 mitered wooden rails. The complex network of converging lattice bars illustrates Leonardo's command of three-dimensional lineation.

Topological Invariants of the Hollow Framework
----------------------------------------------

By the Vacuate Manifold Theorem, the hollow elevated cube framework satisfies:

- **Base Solid Invariants**: :math:`V = 14`, :math:`E = 36`, :math:`F = 24` triangular faces
- **Hollow Frame Vertices**: :math:`V_{\text{vac}} = 2V + 4E = 2(14) + 4(36) = 172`
- **Hollow Frame Edges**: :math:`E_{\text{vac}} = 12E = 12(36) = 432`
- **Hollow Frame Faces**: :math:`F_{\text{vac}} = 6E = 6(36) = 216` planar trapezoids
- **Euler Characteristic**: :math:`\chi_{\text{vac}} = 172 - 432 + 216 = -44`
- **Topological Genus**: :math:`g = F - 1 = 24 - 1 = 23`
- **Coordinate Field**: Pure rational field :math:`\mathbb{Q}`
