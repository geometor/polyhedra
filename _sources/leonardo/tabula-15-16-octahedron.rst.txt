===========================================================
Tabulae XV–XVI: Octahedron Planum Solidum et Vacuum (1509)
===========================================================

*The Regular Octahedron in Solid and Hollow Perspective by Leonardo da Vinci*

.. epigraph::

   *Octahedron planum solidum est corpus octo basibus triangularibus aequilateris contentum...*

   *The solid plane octahedron is a body contained by eight equilateral triangular bases...*

   — **Luca Pacioli**, *De Divina Proportione*, Cap. XXXIII (Venice, 1509)

.. enunciation::

   The regular octahedron :math:`\{3, 4\}` is the Platonic dual of the cube, possessing 6 vertices, 12 edges, and 8 equilateral triangular faces. Leonardo da Vinci illustrates this fundamental cosmic solid in **Tabula XV (Folio 0219)** as *Octahedron Planum Solidum* and in **Tabula XVI (Folio 0221)** as *Octahedron Planum Vacuum*. In hollow form, the eight open triangular apertures create a wooden framework of topological genus :math:`g = F - 1 = 7`.

Air Cosmology & Octahedral Duality
----------------------------------

In Plato's *Timaeus* (54e), the regular octahedron represents **Air** (*Aer*), occupying the intermediate position between Fire (tetrahedron) and Water (icosahedron) in subtlety and mobile weight.

Leonardo renders the solid octahedron resting on one of its equatorial vertices, showcasing four frontal faces converging toward the upper and lower apices.

In Tabula XVI, the hollow carpentry cage features 8 equilateral triangular window openings bounded by 12 mitered wooden rails meeting at 6 vertices. Through the front triangular apertures, the spectator looks directly into the central void and perceives the rear triangular framework.

Topological Invariants of the Hollow Framework
----------------------------------------------

By the Vacuate Manifold Theorem, the hollow octahedron framework satisfies:

- **Base Solid Invariants**: :math:`V = 6`, :math:`E = 12`, :math:`F = 8` equilateral triangular faces
- **Hollow Frame Vertices**: :math:`V_{\text{vac}} = 2V + 4E = 2(6) + 4(12) = 60`
- **Hollow Frame Edges**: :math:`E_{\text{vac}} = 12E = 12(12) = 144`
- **Hollow Frame Faces**: :math:`F_{\text{vac}} = 6E = 6(12) = 72` planar trapezoids
- **Euler Characteristic**: :math:`\chi_{\text{vac}} = 60 - 144 + 72 = -12`
- **Topological Genus**: :math:`g = F - 1 = 8 - 1 = 7`
- **Coordinate Field**: Pure rational field :math:`\mathbb{Q}`
