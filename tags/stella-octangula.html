The Stella Octangula compound of two dual tetrahedra, formed by elevating the eight faces of an octahedron.

Tabulae XIX–XX: Octahedron Elevatum — Stella Octangula (1509)

By erecting regular triangular pyramids over each of the 8 triangular faces of an octahedron, Leonardo da Vinci constructs the celebrated Stella Octangula (Octahedron Elevatum), consisting of \(8 \times 3 = 24\) equilateral triangular faces and \(6 + 8 = 14\) vertices. In Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione (1509), this masterpiece of geometric art is illustrated in Tabula XIX (Folio 0227) as Octahedron Elevatum Solidum and in Tabula XX (Folio 0229) as Octahedron Elevatum Vacuum (genus \(g = 23\)).
