P-03.01 · Rhombic Dodecahedron¶

Dodecahedron Rhombicum · Field: :math:`mathbb{Q}` · Symmetry: O_h (order 48) · Convex

A Catalan solid and space-filling polyhedron bounded by 12 congruent rhombi, polar dual to the cuboctahedron.

Exact Algebraic Coordinates & Geometric Proof¶

The Rhombic Dodecahedron is the polar dual of the semi-regular Archimedean cuboctahedron. Its 14 vertices partition into two distinct orbits under octahedral symmetry \(O_h\):

  • 6 Degree-4 Vertices (axial): placed at \((\pm 3/2, 0, 0)\), \((0, \pm 3/2, 0)\), \((0, 0, \pm 3/2)\) with vertex quadrance \(Q_v = 9/4\).

  • 8 Degree-3 Vertices (cubic corners): placed at \((\pm 3/4, \pm 3/4, \pm 3/4)\) with vertex quadrance \(Q_v = 27/16\).

Each of the 12 faces is a congruent rhombus whose diagonal lengths satisfy:

  • Long Diagonal \(d_1\): connects opposite degree-4 vertices with length \(d_1 = \sqrt{(3/2)^2 + (3/2)^2} = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\).

  • Short Diagonal \(d_2\): connects opposite degree-3 vertices with length \(d_2 = \sqrt{2 \times (3/2)^2} = \frac{3}{2}\).

  • Diagonal Ratio: \(\frac{d_1}{d_2} = \sqrt{2} : 1\) (quadrance ratio \(2 : 1\)).

Because \(\sqrt{2}\) is the ratio of face diagonals, the rhombic dodecahedron packs three-dimensional Euclidean space \(\mathbb{R}^3\) without voids as the Voronoi cell of the face-centered cubic lattice. All vertex coordinates and edge quadrances lie strictly in the rational field \(\mathbb{Q}\).
