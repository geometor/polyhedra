Squaring the Circle & the Earth–Moon Cosmogram

The Great Pyramid of Giza embodies the perimeter dual of squaring the circle: constructing a square pyramid whose square base perimeter equals the circumference of a circle whose radius is the pyramid’s vertical height (\(8a = 2\pi h \iff h/a = 4/\pi\)). Under the Kepler Golden Pyramid profile (\(h = \sqrt{\phi}\)), this relation yields the celebrated algebraic approximation \(\pi \approx 4/\sqrt{\phi} \approx 3.1446\) (\(< 0.1\%\) discrepancy), geometrically realizing the astronomical Earth–Moon tangent cosmogram.
