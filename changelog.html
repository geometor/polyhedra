Changelog¶

0.4.3 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Added universal Dual and Golden φ buttons across all 26 interactive data-solid 3D studios: - Dual (buildFaceCenterDual): Constructs the exact inscribed Face-Center Dual whose vertices are the centroids of every side (rendering depth-cued cyan dual edges and 3D cyan vertex spheres right at the side centers, certifying the self-dual Regular Tetrahedron inside itself). - Golden φ (buildGoldenOverlay): Renders vertex-exact \(1 : \phi\) Golden Rectangles and \(\phi : 1\) Golden Sections (24k gold major segments, coral-rose minor segments, and 3D rose-gold section pearls, including George Odom’s Tetrahedron circumcircle golden section, the 12 golden-divided Octahedron edges, Cube face-flush golden rectangles, pentagonal pentagram sections, and Kepler pyramid apothem altitudes).

0.4.2 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Set Wireframe display mode and 3D Vertices spheres as the default initial view across all 26 interactive data-solid viewports (ordered Wireframe · Surfaces · Frame), while allowing explicit data-mode and data-vertices="false" overrides.

0.4.1 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Overhauled the interactive 3D HTML5 <canvas> studio (polyhedra_3d_codex.js) across all 26 polyhedra and 5 geometric scenes: - Replaced legacy Renaissance/Latin UI labels with modern geometric controls (Surfaces, Wireframe, and Frame display modes, plus Perspective, Isometric, Front, and Top camera presets). - Implemented camera-locked 3-point studio lighting (warm key light, cool sky fill, rim highlight, Fresnel edge sheen, Blinn–Phong specular highlights, radial studio stage backdrop, and luminous theme-aware crease strokes) eliminating back-lit dark facets during 3D orbit. - Added a continuous Opacity (surface transparency) slider (0%–100%) with exact back-to-front painter’s sorting of rear and front facets, alongside a Vertices toggle rendering depth-sorted 3D vertex spheres across all display modes.

0.4.0 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Added geometor.polyhedra.catalog cataloging 26 exact symbolic polyhedra across 5 geometric families (01-platonic, 02-archimedean, 03-catalan, 04-elevated, 05-pyramids), including build_rhombicuboctahedron_solid() (Vigintisex Basium over \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})\)), build_pentagonal_pyramid_solid() (Johnson Solid \(J_2\) / Euclid XIII.16 icosahedral cap over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\)), and uniform Truncated Dodecahedron at \(t = (3 - \phi)/5 \in \mathbb{Q}(\phi)\).

  • Reorganized the entire Book Codex into a one-polyhedron-per-page architecture (docsrc/solids/<family>/<slug>/index.rst) powered by photon-platform/publish .. collection:: grids (Cards / Banners / List switcher, inline .euclid-graphic SVG thumbnails, and automatic /types/, /categories/, and /tags/ taxonomy pages), accompanied by a 26-solid Master Comparative Ledger.

  • Atomized docsrc/leonardo/ into 20 individual Tabula-Pair folio studies (adding Folios 0283/0287 for the Quadrangular Pyramid and 0293/0295 for the Pentagonal Pyramid), split docsrc/golden/ into 5 single-theorem collection pages, and upgraded docsrc/pyramid/ to .. collection::.

  • Extended polyhedra_3d_codex.js so data-solid="<slug>" natively renders all 26 cataloged polyhedra in both Solidum and Vacuum (genus \(g = F - 1\) hollow wooden frame) modes with live aperture control and polar dual wireframes.

0.3.0 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Added geometor.polyhedra.pyramid implementing exact symbolic models over \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\phi})\), \(\mathbb{Q}(\pi)\), and \(\mathbb{Q}\) for the Great Pyramid, 1597 Kepler Right Triangle \((1, \sqrt{\phi}, \phi)\), Squaring the Circle (\(8 = 2\pi h\), \(\pi_{\phi} = 4/\sqrt{\phi}\), \(\pi_{22/7} = 22/7\)), Earth–Moon 11:3 Cosmogram, and Old Kingdom 14:11 Seked mensuration.

  • Certified 11 exact algebraic identities (verify_kepler_pyramid_identities()), including the Herodotus area law (\(A_{\text{face}} = h^2 = \phi\)), continuous golden surface chain (\(A_{\text{base}} : A_{\text{lateral}} : A_{\text{total}} = 1 : \phi : \phi^2\)), Icosahedron circumradius edge bridge (\(Q_{\text{edge}} = \phi + 2\)), and pure \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) / \(\mathbb{Q}\) dihedral spreads (\(3\phi - 4\) and \(85848/100489\)).

  • Added 3D Solid3D builders (build_kepler_pyramid_solid(), build_seked_pyramid_solid(), build_kepler_bipyramid_solid()), 2D geometor.model.Model constructions (build_kepler_triangle_model(), build_pyramid_squaring_circle_model()), interactive 3D scene data-scene="great_pyramid_kepler", 6 .euclid-graphic SVGs, and the dedicated Great Pyramid Book Codex monograph (docsrc/pyramid/).

0.2.1 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Configured top-level header navigation menus (Platonic Solids, Golden Proportions, Leonardo Plates, 3D Pipeline, Log, Modules) with active section highlighting and clean Book Codex breadcrumb inheritance across all child chapters.

0.2.0 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Added exact 3D symbolic modeling kernel geometor.polyhedra.model3d (Point3D, Vector3D, Line3D, Plane3D, Sphere3D, Solid3D) plus 2D Dimensional Bridge (slice_solid_to_model() and unfold_net_2d()).

  • Implemented exact Conway/Pacioli/Leonardo polyhedral operators in geometor.polyhedra.operators: vacuate() (genus \(g = F - 1\) hollow wooden manifold), rectify(), truncate(), elevate() (equilateral stellations including Stella Octangula), and polar_dual().

  • Added geometor.polyhedra.golden proving six canonical Golden Proportion theorems across 3D polyhedra and 2D cross-sections (3 orthogonal golden rectangles in the icosahedron, golden division of the 12 octahedron edges, Euclid XIII.17 compound of 5 cubes in the dodecahedron, Kepler’s 30-face rhombic triacontahedron, Odom’s tetrahedron section, meridian hexagon, and equatorial pentagon/pentagram).

  • Built multi-backend visualization & CAD export suite geometor.polyhedra.render (.euclid-graphic 3D solid/vacuum SVGs, 2D unfolded net SVGs, 2D section SVGs, Wavefront .obj/.mtl, IngeTrazo 0.6.0 live builder, and Blender 5.x bpy studio script) plus zero-dependency interactive HTML5 <canvas> 3D Book Codex engine (polyhedra_3d_codex.js).

  • Authored 15 dedicated :layout: book codex pages across docsrc/platonic/, docsrc/golden/, docsrc/leonardo/ (with 38 curated 1509 Leonardo da Vinci woodcut plates), and docsrc/pipeline/.

0.1.0 (2026-11-14)¶

  • Initial release of geometor-polyhedra (v0.1.0) strictly adhering to GEOMETOR exact symbolic standards (zero float / sp.Float).

  • Implemented exact symbolic Platonic solids registry in geometor.polyhedra.platonic (RegularSolid) with all 5 regular solids (Tetrahedron, Hexahedron, Octahedron, Icosahedron, Dodecahedron) over \(\mathbb{Z}\) and \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\).

  • Defined exact 3D Rational Trigonometry vector helpers: quadrance_3d(), cross_3d(), dot_3d(), face_normal_3d(), and dihedral_spread_3d().

  • Verified polar-duality ratios (\(Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = 1/3\) for Cube and Octahedron, \(Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = (4\phi+3)/15 = 1/3 + 2\sqrt{5}/15\) for Dodecahedron and Icosahedron, \(Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = 1/9\) for self-dual Tetrahedron), all rational dihedral spreads (\(8/9, 1, 8/9, 4/5, 4/9\)), Schläfli counts (\(pF = 2E = qV\)), and Euler characteristic \(V - E + F = 2\).

  • Linked all 60 Leonardo da Vinci polyhedral plates from Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione (1509) across primary variants (solidum, vacuum, abscisum, elevatum).

  • Added polyhedra Rich CLI and runnable demonstration in demos/demo_platonic.py.

  • Authored multi-track Book Codex in docsrc/ with Sphinx AutoAPI reference, Leonardo plates guide, and local intersphinx cross-links.
